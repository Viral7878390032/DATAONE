DATAONE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

DATAONE is a personal data-unification application designed to help you view information from connected accounts in one place. This Privacy Policy explains what information DATAONE handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the services you connect and the features you use, DATAONE may handle:

  • Account information: your DATAONE account identifier and basic account details needed to provide the service.
  • Google account information: the connected Google account's email address and Google account identifier.
  • Google authorization data: OAuth tokens needed to maintain the connection. Refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored in our database.
  • Gmail information: when you use Gmail features, DATAONE may request and display email information and message contents using read-only access.
  • Google Drive information: DATAONE may request file metadata and storage information using read-only access. The current Drive permission does not grant access to download or read file contents.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostic and request information may be processed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How DATAONE uses Google data

DATAONE uses Google data only to provide the features you request, such as showing connected accounts and presenting permitted Gmail or Google Drive information in a unified dashboard.

  • Google access is read-only under the permissions currently requested by DATAONE.
  • DATAONE does not use Google data to send, modify, or delete your emails or Google Drive files.
  • Gmail message contents are fetched when needed for display. DATAONE is designed not to permanently store Gmail message contents in its database. Information may be temporarily processed or held in memory to complete your request.
  • Google Drive access is limited to the metadata permission requested by the app; it does not provide access to the contents of Drive files.

3. Storage and security

DATAONE stores the minimum Google connection information needed to identify your connected account and maintain authorization, including your Google account email, account identifier, and an encrypted refresh token. This connection information is stored in Supabase. The DATAONE website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages.

We use safeguards intended to protect information, including encryption of stored Google refresh tokens and access controls. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or Google user data. We do not share Google user data with advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate DATAONE, such as its database, hosting, and infrastructure providers, only as needed to provide and protect the service, or when disclosure is required by law.

5. Google API Services User Data

DATAONE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are visible and relevant to you. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

6. Retention and deletion

DATAONE retains Google connection information while your Google account remains connected or while it is needed to provide the service. DATAONE does not intend to retain Gmail message contents in its database. You may revoke DATAONE's Google access at any time through your Google Account permissions.

To request deletion of your DATAONE account information or stored Google connection information, contact us using the email address below. We will verify the request and delete the information we are able to delete, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Your choices

  • You choose whether to connect a Google account.
  • You can stop using a connected-account feature at any time.
  • You can revoke Google access from your Google Account permissions page.
  • You can contact us to ask questions or request deletion of stored connection information.

8. Children's privacy

DATAONE is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DATAONE changes. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.
